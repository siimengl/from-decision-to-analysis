# Fictional Sample Project Brief — Chicago Office Performance Study

This is a fictional/sanitized brief created only for the prototype. It is not an M Moser project and contains no client data.

## Decision at stake
The team is deciding which early façade/envelope adjustments are worth developing before the design advances, using an existing five-zone office energy model as the starting point.

## Client / project requirements
- Preserve most of the existing south/front façade glazing and the sense of openness. A major reduction in the primary window opening is undesirable; retain at least 70% of the baseline window area in this early study.
- Reduce cooling-related energy demand where practical and improve overall annual energy performance.
- Avoid a major HVAC system replacement at this stage; focus first on façade/envelope moves that can be studied without redesigning the whole mechanical system.
- Exterior shading may be considered, but very deep or visually dominant projections are less desirable.
- Construction complexity and cost matter. The team prefers targeted changes over a wholesale façade redesign.
- The team wants to understand interactions between measures, not only isolated one-at-a-time results.

## What is not yet known
- Which façade/envelope relationship deserves analysis first.
- Whether reducing window area, increasing shading, or increasing wall insulation produces the most useful benefit in this model.
- Whether measures that look beneficial alone still perform well when combined.
- Whether the current evidence is sufficient to choose a direction or whether another analysis is needed.

## Evidence boundaries
This public EnergyPlus sample is used for energy/thermal evidence only. It does not include a daylight or glare model. Any claim about daylight quality, glare, detailed view quality, embodied carbon, construction cost, or client acceptance must remain a design consideration or `NEEDS EVIDENCE`, not a simulated fact.
